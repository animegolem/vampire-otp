## What this is

performed on it (loom retries, fallback, non-hydration, note-path
surgery), per the DQ-006 settled design (relay topic
`standing-care-recovery-tooling`, all rounds archived). Precedent is

non-transitive in both directions: this directive binds no other
seat, and no other seat's choices amend it.

3. Fallback models are labeled, never silent.
4. Non-hydration is reversible only: preserved manifest, visible

8. Seams recorded as seams — `crash_inferred` epistemics applied to

not mid-incident; revisions are new versions, prior versions
